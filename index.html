About

Hi, I'm Omar.

I'm a Senior Video Editor and Motion Graphics Designer with 5+ years of experience. I handle the whole production, from concept and storyboard through editing and motion graphics to sound design. I make educational and promotional content that gets brands seen and keeps people watching.

I started out as a videographer, so I know lighting and shooting as well as I know the timeline. For the past year I've built AI into my workflow, and I can now take a project from A to Z with it, with more versions and faster turnarounds while I still check every frame by eye.

I've delivered 800+ videos for 100+ clients across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the US, in English and Arabic, for brands and educational platforms including Mastery Academy, File Coffee, Zee Fashion, Med In and Academy 180. As a freelancer I work with clients in education, real estate, coaching and fitness across the US, UAE and Saudi Arabia. I work closely with marketing and art teams, and today I lead a junior editor.
